06 / A few good questions

Before we
say hello.

A little clarity goes a long way.

How long does a project take?

Timing depends on the size of the project and how ready your content is. We’ll agree on a realistic schedule after discussing your requirements.

What do you need from us to get started?

Your goals, a short project brief, examples you like, and any existing brand assets or content. We’ll help you identify what’s missing before we begin.

How do payments and revisions work?

Your proposal will state the payment milestones and included revision rounds. Changes outside the agreed scope will be discussed and quoted before any extra work.

Can you improve an existing website?

Yes, we can review an existing site and discuss a focused update or redesign. What’s possible depends on its platform, current setup, and your access to it.

What happens after the project is delivered?

We hand over the agreed files and access, plus guidance for using your new assets. Any ongoing maintenance or post-launch support is agreed separately.
